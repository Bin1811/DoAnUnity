MEO THU THANH - Cat Tower Defense
================================

Chay game: nhap doi vao MeoThuThanh.exe
Khong can cai Unity. Khong can mang.

Dieu khien:
- Chuot trai: chon / dat meo, bam nut
- Chuot phai hoac Esc: huy dat, bo chon
- Phim 1-4: chon nhanh meo
- Space: goi dot tiep theo
- P: tam dung / tiep tuc
- M: bat tat am thanh
- R: choi lai khi da thang/thua
- Nut x1/x2: tang toc tran dau

Muc tieu: giu thanh meo qua 20 dot chuot.
Moi meo dat tren bai co, khong dat duoc len duong di.
Bam vao meo da dat de nang cap hoac ban.

Chuc ban choi vui!
